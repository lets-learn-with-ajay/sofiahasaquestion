What this covers

This policy covers this website and the publishing tools used to post episodes of "Sofia Has a Question" to the series' own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and similar platforms.

Data we collect

This website collects no personal data, sets no cookies of its own, and runs no analytics.

The publishing tools hold access tokens for the Publisher's own platform accounts only. They do not collect, store or process personal data of viewers or of any other platform user. Public comments left on episodes are read through the platforms' APIs only to post replies from the series' own account; they are not stored beyond the platform itself and are not shared.

Platforms

When you watch or comment on an episode on YouTube or TikTok, that platform's own privacy policy applies to what it collects.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about viewers, there is nothing for us to correct or delete; requests about data held by a platform should go to that platform. For anything else, contact us below.
