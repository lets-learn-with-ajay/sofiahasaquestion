An animated comedy series about real reported UFO cases: what witnesses said, what officials said, and what the visitor, Zib, has to say about it. The characters are fictional; no real person is depicted.

Watch on YouTube: @SofiaHasaQuestion.

This site also hosts the pages required for the series' publishing tools, which post each day's episode to the series' own social accounts.
